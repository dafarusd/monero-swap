Monero for ETH, no middleman

Pick an offer, pay ETH into a contract, get Monero. The seller can't take your ETH without handing over the Monero, and if they vanish, your ETH comes back by itself. This page runs in your browser and keeps your keys here. It never sends them anywhere.

Keys for your swaps are stored in this browser only. Download the backup when offered — if you lose this browser, that file is how you restore the swap and reach your Monero. Without it, clearing site data before a swap finishes loses the Monero.
